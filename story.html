ADM Kochi was founded in 2010 with a simple conviction: Kerala deserved a workshop that treated a 7-Series the way a 7-Series is treated in Munich. The early years were about earning that right — bay by bay, customer by customer, learning the mechanical and electrical idioms of each marque from the inside.

Fifteen years on, that workshop is now an ecosystem. A dedicated body shop. A climate-controlled care bay. A diagnostics room wired for OEM-tier scan tools. And a live-floor training programme that turns aspiring technicians into specialists — not through lectures, but through weeks on the bay.

Our founder brings years of hands-on experience with Rolls-Royce, BMW and other premium marques. That expertise now flows into every diagnostic, every repair, and every student who walks the floor.

The vision is clear: to become the leading automotive ecosystem in South India — a place where luxury car owners and aspiring technicians meet the same standard of precision.
