## Notes
- Preferred aspect ratio: **16:9** for tiles, **4:3** for hero panels
- Resolution: minimum **1400px wide**
- Style: **clean, editorial, premium** — not stock-photo-generic
- Tools to generate: Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Adobe Firefly, or hire a local photographer
